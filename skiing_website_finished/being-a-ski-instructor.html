Being a Ski Instructor

I am also a ski instructor at Discovery Ski Area. I became an instructor because I love teaching and helping other people learn how to ski.

One of my favorite parts of being an instructor is seeing young kids get out on the mountain, learn new skills, and have fun skiing. It is rewarding to watch them become more confident and enjoy the mountain.
